Positions

Equity delta = δ × parity × nominal, in EUR. Gamma = change in equity delta for a +1% stock move. Vega per vol point, CS01 per +1 bp of spread (index Rho). DV01 = −MV × duration × 1 bp, per +1 bp of rates, duration from the index file (BgAM if missing). Theta per calendar day = gamma decay −½ × IV² × parity × Γ / 365 (the breakeven of the gamma term used in the attribution) + coupon accrual; an estimate, not the pricing model's theta. Index options: Black-Scholes gamma, vega and theta (zero rates and dividends) from BgAM delta and IV, with expiry read from the instrument name and d1 backed out of the delta. Greeks from the index file at each date; bonds missing from the index fall back to BgAM delta and parity (no gamma/vega/CS01). Index options and futures enter delta at their delta-adjusted exposure.

Each CB is modelled as its current price plus a Black-Scholes call on parity calibrated to the index delta and gamma (d1 from δ, total vol from Γ, strike implied); index options use BgAM delta, IV and expiry; futures are linear. Static: no time decay, no vol or spread change, every stock moves by the same percentage (beta 1). Max convexity is the move at which the composite gamma peaks. "Of which convexity" is the CB P&L beyond today's delta × move.

Index options and futures carry a delta-adjusted exposure in the report, so their P&L needs the underlying level at T0 (and at T1 for the delta/other split). Futures are priced from the report directly. S&P underlyings are pre-filled from the ES future as a proxy; edit the cells.

Same decomposition applied to every constituent of the index file, weighted by index weight at T0, in local currency on clean prices plus coupon accrual. Approximates the index's hedged return; constituents missing on one date are dropped.

For each convertible held at T0, nominal in bond currency is recovered from the exposure report (N = Exposure / Price% × EUR-ccy spot). The dirty-price move from the fund's own valuation gives the P&L; the clean-price move in the index file is explained with the index greeks; the two are reconciled through carry and a pricing-basis term, so every line sums exactly to the valuation P&L.

Equity
δ × Parity₀ × (S₁/S₀ − 1) — stock move in its own currency.
Equity FX
δ × ΔParity − Equity — the part of the parity move not explained by the stock (cross-currency bonds, ratio adjustments).
Gamma
Model: ½ × Γ × Parity₀ × r² with Γ the index gamma (delta points per 1% parity move) and r the parity return. Realized alternative: ½ × (δ₁ − δ₀) × ΔParity.
Vega
Vega × ΔIV (price points per vol point). Ignored when IV is missing on either date or moves by more than the threshold, which usually signals a model re-calibration on a low-delta bond.
Credit
ΔSpread/10 × Rho(10bp). Rho is the discount-rate sensitivity of the index model and is used as the spread proxy.
Rates
Δr/10 × Rho(10bp), with Δr per currency from the settings (default 0).
Carry
Coupon accrual, coupon × days/365; the index clean price excludes it, the fund's dirty price includes it.
Residual
Index clean-price move minus the explained components: theta, bond-floor accretion, cross terms and index pricing noise.
Pricing basis
Fund clean-price move minus index clean-price move: the difference between the two pricing sources (time of snapshot, dealer marks).
FX translation
N₀ × Dirty₀ × (1/FX₁ − 1/FX₀), shown as a memo only: positions are FX-hedged with forwards, so it is excluded from attributed P&L.
Trades
A change in nominal between T0 and T1 is assumed to trade at the T1 price (zero P&L on the traded quantity) and flagged. A position sold in full uses the index price at T1 as exit proxy.

Units: the index file quotes the bond price in % of par, parity in bond-currency points, delta in %, spread in bp, Vega per vol point, Rho per 10 bp. AUM is derived from the exposure report's total line (exposure ÷ % of AUM). The "T0/T1" labels follow the file dates; both sources published on the same date reflect the same close.

How it works

  1. Drop two BgAM Exposure Reports (consecutive dates) and the two UCBINDEX constituent files for the same dates.
  2. Positions are matched to the index by ISIN (RegS, 144A and registered lines included), nominals are recovered from exposure ÷ price.
  3. Valuation P&L is split into equity, gamma, vega, credit, rates, carry, residual and pricing basis; index options and futures sit in a separate overlay bucket.
  4. The same decomposition on the whole index gives a benchmark line to compare against.

No data is uploaded anywhere; parsing and calculation run in the page. Use "Load sample data" to see the layout with a fictitious five-bond book.
